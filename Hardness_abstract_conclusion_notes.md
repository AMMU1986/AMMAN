### Short note for the ABSTRACT (hardness profile)

A continuous microhardness traverse across the base metal (BM), heat-affected zone (HAZ) and fusion zone (FZ) revealed a progressive rise in hardness with electrode strength grade. The FZ was consistently the hardest region (238 HV for E6018, 252 HV for E7018 and 268 HV for E9018) against a uniform BM value of 245 HV, while the corresponding HAZ hardness followed the same order (225, 232 and 241 HV). The hardest E9018 weld, governed by its fine acicular ferrite structure, coincided with the lowest mass loss and no bend-test cracking, confirming the direct link between microhardness, microstructural refinement and intergranular corrosion resistance.

### Short note for the CONCLUSION (hardness profile)

- The microhardness profile increased in the order E6018 < E7018 < E9018 in both the FZ and the HAZ, with the FZ always harder than the HAZ and the BM maintaining a steady 245 HV.
- The E9018 joint recorded the highest hardness (268 HV FZ, 241 HV HAZ) owing to its fine acicular ferrite, giving a smooth BM–HAZ–FZ transition that minimised local property mismatch.
- The hardness ranking mirrored the intergranular corrosion and bend-test results, confirming that the higher-strength E9018 electrode delivers the best combined mechanical and corrosion performance for API X70 SMAW joints.
